Technology

5-Stage Advanced
Water Purification

Every drop undergoes rigorous multi-stage treatment for guaranteed purity — consistent across every single bottle.

01
🔵
Pre-Ozonation
Ozone eliminates bacteria, viruses, and harmful germs before the filtration process begins — a powerful first line of defence.
→
02
🪨
Sand Filtration
Removes dirt, sand, and all visible particles from the water, ensuring physical clarity at the source.
→
03
⚫
Activated Carbon
Eliminates chlorine, bad odour, unpleasant taste, and harmful chemicals — delivering clean, fresh water.
→
04
💧
Reverse Osmosis
Removes salts, heavy metals, and microscopic impurities. Makes water highly pure with TDS below 50 ppm.
→
05
☀️
UV Disinfection
UV light destroys any remaining microorganisms — the final guarantee of 100% safe drinking water in every bottle.
