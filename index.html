Technology

5-Stage Advanced
Water Purification

Every drop undergoes rigorous multi-stage treatment for guaranteed purity.

🔵01
Pre-Ozonation
Ozone eliminates bacteria, viruses and harmful germs before filtration begins.
🏔️02
Sand Filtration
Removes dirt, sand, and all visible particles from the water source.
⚫03
Activated Carbon
Removes chlorine, bad odour, taste, and harmful chemicals completely.
💧04
Reverse Osmosis
Eliminates salts, heavy metals and micro-impurities for ultra-low TDS.
☀️05
UV Disinfection
UV light destroys any remaining germs ensuring 100% safe drinking water.
